Who We Are

Vasunnati Foundation is a non-profit organization focused on promoting civic responsibility and sustainability through structured education, awareness programs, and community-driven initiatives, with a strong emphasis on youth engagement.

1. Civic Sense

Civic sense is awareness and practice of responsible behavior in public spaces, based on respect for others and environment. It includes everyday actions like cleanliness, following rules, and contributing positively, creating safer, organized, and sustainable communities for all more inclusive future.

2. Sustainability

We are making choices today that protects and preserves our planet for the future. We are keen about using resources wisely, reducing waste, and living in a way that keeps our environment clean, balanced, and thriving for generations to come.

What We Do

We design and implement educational programs and community-driven initiatives focused on civic sense and sustainability. Our work includes conducting awareness sessions in schools, organizing cleanliness and environmental drives, promoting responsible public behavior, and engaging communities through practical, action-oriented campaigns that create lasting social impact.

Cleanliness Drive

We conduct structured cleanliness drives by mobilizing volunteers and collaborating with local communities to restore and maintain public spaces.

Waste Segregation

We promote waste segregation at source by educating communities to separate dry, wet, and hazardous waste effectively.

Civic Sense Awareness

We conduct civic sense awareness sessions in schools, focusing on shaping responsible behavior from an early age.

About Our Journey

Established in July 2025, Vassunnati Foundation has been consistently working towards promoting civic sense, cleanliness, and sustainability at the grassroots level. Through structured sessions in government and private schools, as well as colleges, we aim to instill responsible civic behavior among the youth. In parallel, our on-ground efforts include cleanliness drives and awareness initiatives at key locations such as Ralamandal, Mitha Talab (Dewas), Bilawli Lake, and Kishanpura Lake. Our journey reflects a committed effort to create cleaner spaces and foster a more aware and responsible community.

Vision
To build a conscious and responsible society where civic sense, cleanliness, and sustainable living are deeply ingrained in everyday behavior, creating healthier communities and a greener planet.
Mission
To create lasting change by educating and empowering youth to adopt responsible civic habits, promote cleanliness, and practice sustainability through awareness, community engagement, and action initiatives.
